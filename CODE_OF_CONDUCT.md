# Code of conduct — X Messenger

We follow the Contributor Covenant v2.1.

Be kind. No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
No sharing of other people's private data or secrets.
Security reports stay private until fixed.

Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

Problems: contact the repository owner via GitHub. The owner may remove
comments, close issues, or block accounts to protect the community.
